Bio

Jinho Byun is a Research Professor in the Department of Energy Engineering at KENTECH. He earned his Ph.D. in Physics from Pusan National University, focusing on emergent phenomena in polar oxides and their applications. His research interests include deep learning, ab initio multislice simulation, ab initio molecular dynamics, machine‑learning force fields, and phonon properties, with materials focus on oxide surfaces/interfaces, perovskites, and 2D materials.
